<Info>
  Once you have created an organisation in disQo.ai, the Organisation section will appear in your Settings. Here, you can manage your organisation details, team members, billing, and more.
</Info>

## Accessing Organisation Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Organisation">
    Click your profile menu and select  to start a new organisation for your team.

    <Frame caption="Create Organisation menu">
      ![Settings Organisation My Org](/images/Settings---Organisation---My-Org.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Organisation Details">
    After creation, access  in Settings to update your organisation name, avatar, and slug.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Team Members">
    Use the  section to invite, view, and manage your team. Assign roles and manage permissions.

    <Frame caption="Team Members management UI">
      ![Settings Team Members](/images/Settings--Team-Members.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Billing and Subscription">
    The  section allows you to view your subscription plan, manage payments, and see your team size and next payment date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Danger Zone">
    In Danger Zone, you can leave, delete, or transfer your organisation. Use caution—deleting an organisation is irreversible.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Organisation management features are only available after you have created an organisation. Billing and team management options will also appear in your Settings menu.
</Tip>
