<Info>
  The Settings area in disQo.ai allows you to:

  • Manage your personal account

  • Configure your subscription

  • Set up integrations

  • Manage your team and billing (if part of an organisation)

  All settings are accessible from the sidebar for easy navigation.
</Info>

<Frame caption="Settings page UI">
  ![Settings Organisation My Org](/images/Settings---Organisation---My-Org.png)
</Frame>

## What You Can Manage in Settings

-  (Visible after creating an organisation)
  -  Update organisation details and avatar.
  -  Invite, view, and manage your team.
  -  Manage your organisation's subscription and payments.
  -  Leave, delete, or transfer your organisation.
  <Note>
    You can create an organisation directly from the Settings area. For step-by-step instructions, see the  page.
  </Note>
-  Switch between light and dark mode.
-  Log out of your account securely.

<Tip>
  Organisation management, team, and billing options will only appear after you have created or joined an organisation.
</Tip>
